# Brief 045: PLC racks and cards

Part of the [quick start](../quick-start.md). Design: `docs/design/045.md`; briefs 045a, 045b and
045c, built in that order on `feature/plc`.

## 1. Rack and cards (045a)

### What this part changed

| Where | What is there now |
| --- | --- |
| Find Symbol ▸ PLC | Three new bodies: `plcim` (PLC head module, a rack of its own, for a remote I/O station), `plcpm` (power module) and `plccm` (communication module) |
| Place Symbol | Placing a card body (`plcdi`, `plcdq`, `plcai`, `plcao`, `plcrtd`, `plciol`, `plcps`, `plcpm`, `plccm`) when the project has a rack asks **Rack** (the rack of the last card placed, else the only rack; `None` for no rack) and **Slot** (the next free one), then the insertion point. Enter, Enter takes both. A taken slot is refused with the card there and the next free slot. Placing a `plccpu` or `plcim` makes a rack; it asks nothing |
| The page | A card in a slot has a caption under its body: `-A01 slot 2` (Settings ▸ Standards ▸ PLC: **Card caption**, **Show the card caption**) |
| Properties ▸ device | A card shows **Rack** (a choice of the project's racks and None, by tag), **Slot** and **Channels**; a rack shows **Slots** and **First slot** |
| Checks | `Two cards in one slot` (**Move to slot n**), `Card names a rack that is not in the project` (**Put in rack…**, **Take out of rack**), `Card in a slot its rack does not have` (**Raise slots**, **Move to free slot**), and one count row `n PLC cards are in no rack` (**Put in rack…**) |
| Delete | Deleting a rack keeps its cards and takes them out of it, in the same step: `Deleted -A01; 6 cards are now in no rack.` U puts both back |
| Commands | `plcslot {card, rack?, slot?, swap?}` moves, swaps or takes out a card; `plcracks` lists each rack's slots |

### Try it

1. New project. Find Symbol, type `plccpu`, click it onto the page. Expected: `-A01`.
2. Place Symbol `plcdi`. Expected: the prompt asks `Rack <-A01>`; press Enter. It asks
   `Slot <1>`; press Enter, then click on the page. Expected: `Placed -A02 (DI card) in -A01 slot
   1.` and the caption `-A01 slot 1` under the body.
3. Place two more cards (`plcai`, `plcdq`) the same way. Expected: slots 2 and 3.
4. Select `-A03`. In Properties, change **Slot** to `1`. Expected: refused, naming `-A02` and the
   next free slot. Type `5`. Expected: the caption reads `-A01 slot 5`.
5. Rename `-A01` to `-A10` in Properties. Expected: every caption reads `-A10 slot n`.
6. Delete `-A10`. Expected: `Deleted -A10; 3 cards are now in no rack.`; Checks has `3 PLC cards
   are in no rack`. Press U. Expected: the rack is back and every card is in its slot.

Limitations: no rack overview or PLC manager yet (2.8).

## 2. Channels and addresses (045b)

### What this part changed

| Where | What is there now |
| --- | --- |
| Find Symbol ▸ PLC | Channel cells `chdi`, `chdq`, `chai`, `chao`, `chrtd`, `chiol`: one channel of a card, drawn as a cell; cells side by side read as the card. `di` and `do` stay as compact DI and DQ channels |
| Place Symbol | A cell or `di`/`do` with **Device** joins that card as its lowest channel not drawn yet (or `channel`); on its own it makes a new card of its type. A wrong type, a full card or a drawn channel is refused with the way out. On a wire, its first point lands on the wire |
| Pins | A channel point's label is its **pin** (`-A02:4`): role by role (`+` 1–4, `-` 5–8 on a 4-channel AI) or, with `plc.pins channel`, channel by channel. Pins are given once and never renumbered |
| The page | Each cell shows its channel number, its address and its description written upwards under it (`Spare` on a drawn spare channel) |
| Addresses | Live by slot and channel per rack: `byte.bit` (`I2.3`, `IW4`), `iec` (`%IX2.3`), `slot` (`I:3/04`) or `none`. A typed address wins (`*` in lists). Moving a card shifts the cards after it; `plcaddress fix` pins a whole rack |
| Properties ▸ device | On a cell: Channel, Description, Address (faint: the automatic one), a Pin row per role |
| Checks | `Channel drawn twice` (**Move to channel n**), `Channel beyond the card's count` (**Raise to n channels**), `Channel drawn as another card type` (go-to), `Address blocks overlap` (**Move after**, **Unpin**), `Address given twice` (**Clear the typed address**), `Address block past byte 65535` (**Unpin**), `Used channels with no address` (count) |
| Commands (Ctrl+K: Place channels, Channel data, PLC addresses) | `plcchannels {card?, type?, from, to}` (a fence across wires) and `{card, rest: true}` (draw the rest as spares); `plcchannel {card, channel, text?, address?, pins?}`; `plcaddress {rack, style?, input_start?, output_start?, fix?, clear?}` or `{card, start?, size?}` |

### Try it

1. On the project of section 1, draw eight vertical wires 10 mm apart under some lamps.
2. Ctrl+K ▸ **Place channels**, fill the template `plcchannels {"card": "-A02", "from": [x0, y],
   "to": [x1, y]}` with a line across the wires. Expected: `Placed -A02 channels 0–7 on 8 wires
   (pins 1–8).`, cells with `0`…`7`, `I0.0`…`I0.7`.
3. `plcchannels {"card": "-A02", "rest": true}`. Expected: eight more cells, `Spare` under each.
4. Select a cell; in Properties type a **Description**. Expected: it is written upwards under the
   cell.
5. Settings ▸ Standards ▸ PLC: set **Address style** to `iec`. Expected: every address reads
   `%IX…`.
6. Move `-A02` to slot 5 (Properties ▸ Slot). Expected: the echo says how many cards' addresses
   change, and the cells show the new addresses.

Limitations: the card wheel rows (Place channels, Draw remaining channels, Slot…, Addresses…) are
Ctrl+K actions for now; the device wheel has no free slot. A cell on a page without the card body
does not yet show the body's place, and the optional channel table (`plc.xref.table`) is not drawn.
