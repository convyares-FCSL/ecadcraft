# Brief 045: PLC racks and cards

Part of the [quick start](../quick-start.md). Design: `docs/design/045.md`; briefs 045a, 045b and
045c, built in that order on `feature/plc`.

## 1. Rack and cards (045a)

### What this part changed

| Where | What is there now |
| --- | --- |
| Find Symbol ▸ PLC | Three new bodies: `plcim` (PLC head module, a rack of its own, for a remote I/O station), `plcpm` (power module) and `plccm` (communication module) |
| Place Symbol | Placing a card body (`plcdi`, `plcdq`, `plcai`, `plcao`, `plcrtd`, `plciol`, `plcps`, `plcpm`, `plccm`) when the project has a rack asks **Rack** (the rack of the last card placed, else the only rack; `None` for no rack) and **Slot** (the next free one), then the insertion point. Enter, Enter takes both. A taken slot is refused with the card there and the next free slot. Placing a `plccpu` or `plcim` makes a rack; it asks nothing |
| The page | A card in a slot has a caption under its body: `-A01 slot 2` (Settings ▸ Standards ▸ PLC: **Card caption**, **Show the card caption**) |
| Properties ▸ device | A card shows **Rack** (a choice of the project's racks and None, by tag), **Slot** and **Channels**; a rack shows **Slots** and **First slot** |
| Checks | `Two cards in one slot` (**Move to slot n**), `Card names a rack that is not in the project` (**Put in rack…**, **Take out of rack**), `Card in a slot its rack does not have` (**Raise slots**, **Move to free slot**), and one count row `n PLC cards are in no rack` (**Put in rack…**) |
| Delete | Deleting a rack keeps its cards and takes them out of it, in the same step: `Deleted -A01; 6 cards are now in no rack.` U puts both back |
| Commands | `plcslot {card, rack?, slot?, swap?}` moves, swaps or takes out a card; `plcracks` lists each rack's slots |

### Try it

1. New project. Find Symbol, type `plccpu`, click it onto the page. Expected: `-A01`.
2. Place Symbol `plcdi`. Expected: the prompt asks `Rack <-A01>`; press Enter. It asks
   `Slot <1>`; press Enter, then click on the page. Expected: `Placed -A02 (DI card) in -A01 slot
   1.` and the caption `-A01 slot 1` under the body.
3. Place two more cards (`plcai`, `plcdq`) the same way. Expected: slots 2 and 3.
4. Select `-A03`. In Properties, change **Slot** to `1`. Expected: refused, naming `-A02` and the
   next free slot. Type `5`. Expected: the caption reads `-A01 slot 5`.
5. Rename `-A01` to `-A10` in Properties. Expected: every caption reads `-A10 slot n`.
6. Delete `-A10`. Expected: `Deleted -A10; 3 cards are now in no rack.`; Checks has `3 PLC cards
   are in no rack`. Press U. Expected: the rack is back and every card is in its slot.

Limitations: no rack overview or PLC manager yet (2.8).
