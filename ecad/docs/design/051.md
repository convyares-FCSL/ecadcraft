### 0.2 The target, from the mock-up and the pack (patterns only)

**Mock-up, screen m** (`ECADCraft UI design.pdf`, page 16): the PLC tab of Lists shows the rack as a
row of tiles, one per slot (CPU first, a free slot last): slot number, tag, a one-line summary, a
square per channel (in use, free, provisional address, has a check), and a link line at the foot
(the page, "not drawn yet", or "page n · 1 check"). The selected tile has an accent border. A legend
and a total ("n channels, m in use") sit under the row; the selected card's channel table under
that.

**The owner's pack** (confidential, `ecad/private/`, read for patterns; nothing of it is in this
repo, and no text from it is quoted here):

- A card is **one long box**. Its connection points sit **on the top edge**; each wire **ends at the
  point** (a dot on the edge). Nothing crosses the box.
- **Short dividers** drop from the top edge between channels, about a quarter of the box height. The
  pin number sits just under the point, centred; on multi-point channels the role name sits under
  each pin.
- Under the dividers, centred on each channel, a **small mark** of the channel's kind; on some cards
  an address line above the mark.
- The lower part of the box is open. **Bottom left inside:** a name block of three lines: the card's
  name by rack and slot, the maker, the part number. **Bottom right inside:** the card's kind and
  rating in one line.
- The card's own supply points (24 V and 0 V) are the **last cells** of the same box, at its right
  end.
- Channel functions are written **rotated 90°** outside the box, one per channel, reading upwards,
  their ends near the box; unused channels read **Spare**.
- Pitch: 20 mm per single-point channel; 10 mm between the points of a multi-point channel.
- Output cards are drawn **upside down**: points on the bottom edge, wires leaving downwards, the
  functions above the box, the name block top left.
- A card drawn **in part** ends in a **slanted edge** on the side where it continues.
- Report sheets (parts lists, cable and terminal lists): per group a small heading line above a
  **fully ruled table** (outer box, every column and row line), the column header row inside the
  box, groups following each other on the same sheet.

## 1. Goal and words

A PLC card on the page looks like the card in the cabinet and in the owner's pack: one box sized to
its channels, wires ending on its top edge, pins and functions readable at print size, and its name,
maker and part inside it. Lists ▸ PLC opens on the rack as screen m shows it. The I/O list and a rack
overview print as sheets of the book in the pack's report look.

| Word | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **run** | Cells (and a card body) of **one card** on one page, abutting along one line, in one rotation. A run is drawn as one box |
| **frame** | The derived outline of a run: its sides, its bottom edge, the name block and the type text. Not stored |
| **power end** | A card body (`plcdi` …) redrawn as the last cell of its card: the card's supply points on the top edge |
| **break edge** | A slanted side of a frame where the card continues (channels not in this run) |
| **name block** | Inside the frame, bottom left: tag, caption (rack and slot), maker, part number |
| **tile** | One slot of the rack in Lists ▸ PLC and on the `plcrack` sheet |

The glossary gains "run (of a card)", "frame", "power end", "break edge", "tile" in the PLC section.

## 2. The card on the page

### 2.1 Anatomy (a DI card of 16 channels, drawn whole, at 0°)

```
        wire     wire     wire                                          wire  wire
         |        |        |                                             |     |
  +------o--------o--------o----//----o--------o-------------------------o-----o------+  <- top edge, points on it
  |      1   |    2   |    3   |    |   15   |   16   |                 L+  |  M    |    pin under each point
  |          |        |        |    |        |        |                     |       |  <- dividers, header band (10 mm)
  |0  I0.0   |1 I0.1  |2 I0.2  |    |14 I1.6 |15 I1.7 |                             |    channel number, address
  |    >     |   >    |   >    |    |   >    |   >    |                             |    channel mark
  |                                                                                  |  <- mark band (15 mm)
  | -A03                                                                             |
  | -A01 slot 2                                                                      |
  | <maker>                                                                          |
  | <part number>                                                 DI 16  <or its description>
  +----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+  <- bottom edge (name band ≥ 12.5 mm)
     P        P        S                     S        S
```

Every measure below is in millimetres at print size; y up; local frame of the cell (it rotates and
mirrors with the symbol).

### 2.2 Cells (definitions redrawn; `symbols.rs`)

Points keep 045's local coordinates (y = 5, so no placed wire moves), the box moves up so the
**top edge is the line of the points**. Widths follow the pack's pitch: 20 for a single-point
channel, 10 between the points of a multi-point channel.

| Key | Width (was) | Points (role at x, y 5) | Mark (centred at x 0, y −13) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `chdi` | **20** (10) | `in` 0 | arrowhead into the card, 3 wide |
| `chdq` | **20** (10) | `out` 0 | arrowhead out of the card |
| `chai` | 20 | `+` −5, `-` 5 | inward arrowhead over a tilde |
| `chao` | 20 | as `chai` | outward arrowhead over a tilde |
| `chrtd` | 40 | `I+` −15, `S+` −5, `S-` 5, `I-` 15 | a small resistor box with a slash (4 × 2) |
| `chiol` | 30 | `L+` −10, `C/Q` 0, `L-` 10 | a small diamond (3 × 3) |

A cell's **graphics** (the definition) are only:

- its share of the top edge, from (−w/2, 5) to (w/2, 5);
- its two dividers, from (±w/2, 5) down to (±w/2, −5) (the header band, 10 deep);
- its mark.

No bottom edge, no leads, no role text in the graphics (the role names become texts, §2.5). Adjacent
cells share their divider lines (drawn twice, same place). The marks are new drawings for ECAD
(034 §2.1's rules: mm, no arcs), not copied from anywhere.

**Spacing.** The fence (`plcchannels`) needs crossings at least one cell width apart. Closer
crossings are refused before anything is written, naming the wires and the way out: `Wires 24V-12
and 24V-13 are 10 mm apart; a DI cell is 20 mm wide. Space the wires 20 mm apart, or place compact
di symbols.` (Q1). Wires exactly one width apart give abutting cells, so a row of wires on the 20 mm
pitch becomes one card.

Compact `di` and `do` (018) are unchanged and get no frame: they are the compact form.

### 2.3 Runs

A **run** is a maximal set of representations on one page that all belong to one card and:

- are channel cells of that card, or its card body (§2.4);
- have the same rotation and mirror;
- have their top edges on one line (within `text.run.align`, 1 mm, 031's entry) and abut: the gap
  between one's edge and the next one's edge is at most 0.5 mm.

Runs are found per page from `Plc`'s `by_page` index (cells on that page) plus the bodies on the
page: sort along the top line, split where the gap or the line breaks. At most 256 channel cells
plus one body per card per page are read (045's bound); more is already a Checks row.

### 2.4 Card bodies as the power end (definitions redrawn)

The card bodies with channels (`plcdi`, `plcdq`, `plcai`, `plcao`, `plcrtd`, `plciol`) keep their
points and their IDs. Their outline becomes **one power cell**: top edge from (−10, 25) to (10, 25)
through `L+` (−5, 25) and `M` (5, 25), dividers at x ±10 from y 25 to 15. So:

- placed abutting a run's end (left or right), on the run's top line: it **is** the run's last (or
  first) cell, as in the pack;
- placed anywhere else (the rack page, a power page): it is a run of its own and gets its own frame
  and name block, so it still reads as the card;
- the type word (`DI`) is no longer graphics: it is the frame's type text (§2.6).

Bodies without channels (`plccpu`, `plcim`, `plcps`, `plcpm`, `plccm`) are redrawn the same way:
the top edge through their points, dividers between neighbouring points, width = span of the
points + 10, at least 20. They always form a run of their own.

### 2.5 Bands and texts (all from text styles)

Every height below is the **resolved height of the text style** named (031 kinds today, 050 styles
when it lands); positions are computed from those heights, never fixed sizes, so a house style that
raises a height moves the texts apart instead of on top of each other.

| Band (from the top edge down) | Depth | Texts in it | Style | Placement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Header | 10 (fixed: the dividers) | pin at each point | `point_label` (1.5) | centred under the point, top 1 below the edge |
| | | role name under each pin (multi-point cells only: `+`, `S-`, `C/Q` …) | `point_label` | centred, top 1 below the pin |
| Mark | 15 (fixed: the mark at its centre) | channel number | `point_label` | left, top 1 below the dividers, 1 in from the cell's left |
| | | address | `address` (**new**, 2.0) | centred on the cell, top 1 below the dividers |
| Name | max(12.5, name block + 2) rounded up to 2.5 | name block, §2.6 | `tag`, then `part_number` (1.6) per line | left, 2 in from the frame side, bottom 1 above the bottom edge |
| | | type text | `part_number` | right, 2 in from the frame side, bottom 1 above the bottom edge |
| Outside | — | channel text (description, or `Spare`) | `channel_text` (**new**, 2.5) | rotated 90°, reading upwards, centred on the cell, its end 2 below the bottom edge; at `plc.text.channel.rotation 0`: horizontal, centred, top 2 below the bottom edge |

**No overlap, by construction:** the bands are stacked, texts in one band are placed side by side
by cell, and the widths are checked with the font (`text_style::text_width`):

- A pin or role wider than its point's share (10 for multi-point cells) or an address or channel
  number pair wider than the cell minus 2 is **still drawn**, and the run gets one Info row in Checks
  `plc-text-wide`: `-A03: addresses are wider than the 20 mm cells at 2.0 mm (Settings ▸ Standards ▸
  Text styles ▸ address)`, go-to the run. Nothing is shrunk silently (the style is the user's).
- If the style heights make the header texts deeper than 10 or the mark-band texts deeper than 15,
  the same row says so. At the built-in heights everything fits with room (pin + role = 1 + 1.5 + 1 +
  1.5 = 5 of 10; address line + mark = 1 + 2 + 2 + 3 = 8 of 15).
- 031's text-on-wire avoidance still applies to the outside channel texts.

Shown or not: 045's entries `plc.text.channel.show`, `plc.text.address.show`, `plc.text.caption.show`
are unchanged. Role names follow `text.point_label.show` (031).

Texts are derived (`plc::channel_texts` for the cells, `look` for the frame), as today: nothing is
stored, and the inspector's text overrides (031 moved texts) keep working for channel texts.

### 2.6 The frame

For each run `look` draws, in `sheet.ink` at 040's symbol line weight:

- **Sides:** from the top edge to the bottom edge at the run's two ends. A side is a **break edge**
  (the bottom end 5 mm further out than the top, a slant) when the card continues beyond it: the
  left side when the run's first channel is not the card's first, the right side when its last
  channel is not the card's last (a power end counts as after the last channel). A run that is only
  a body has straight sides.
- **Bottom edge:** across the run, at the depth of §2.5 (header + mark + name band).
- **Width:** the run's width, widened to the right (the right side then a break edge) when the name
  block plus the type text plus 6 would not fit. So a single cell still has a readable name block.
- **Name block** (lines skipped when empty or switched off):
  1. the tag (`-A03`) in `tag`, when `text.tag.show` shows it for this card (031; the house style of
     the pack hides it and uses the caption);
  2. the caption (`plc.card.caption`, `-A01 slot 2`), when the card is in a rack and
     `plc.text.caption.show` is on;
  3. the maker (`maker` property);
  4. the part number (`part_number` property).
- **Type text:** the card's description when it has one, else `<type> <channels>` (`DI 16`).
- **Tags once per card** (031's `text.tag.show.plc`, `first`): the name block is the card's tag
  place on that page; 045's separate tag above the body and caption under it are no longer drawn
  for a body inside a run (§2.5 replaces them). A run on a page without the body still has its name
  block.

`plc.frame.show` (§6, on) switches the frame off for a house style that draws its own; then the
cells' top edge and dividers stand alone, and texts keep their bands.

### 2.7 Rotation (output cards upside down)

A run rotated 180° (the pack's outputs: points on the bottom edge, wires leaving downwards) is laid
out in its own frame and then turned, with one rule for its texts: every text stays upright (031's
readable rotation), the **name block goes to the left and the type text to the right** of the far
side (so the name block is top left, as in the pack), and the channel texts sit outside the far
edge, reading upwards, their ends 2 from the frame. At 90° and 270° the same rule, reading from the
bottom or the right per 031. Mirror: the layout mirrors; texts never do.

### 2.8 Wires end at the point

**Fence and single placement on a wire** (`plcchannels`, Place Symbol of a cell on a wire), in the
same transaction as the cells (028's split ops plus 018's conductor delete):

1. The wire is split at the cell's point, as today.
2. The piece on the card's side of the point (inside the frame's rectangle as laid out for the new
   run) is **removed** when its far end is loose or ends inside that rectangle.
3. If it goes on beyond the rectangle to another connection point, it is kept (connectivity is never
   changed silently), the echo says so, and Checks shows a Warning `wire-through-card`: `Wire
   24V-12 runs through -A03 channel 3 to -K03:A1`, go-to the wire. No automatic fix.

Echo: `Placed -A03 channels 0–7 on 8 wires (pins 1–8); trimmed 8 wire ends.` Undo restores the
pieces. So the wire's label (022) is always on the wire above the card, never in it (W2), and no
`Loose wire` row comes from a fence (W3).

Cells already drawn by 045 with wire pieces through them are not rewritten; the pieces show as 022's
`Loose wire` rows with 022's fix, or as `wire-through-card`.

### 3.2 Rack chips

A segmented control (037's primitive) above the strip: one chip per rack (`-A01 CPU`, its tag and
type), in tag order, and `No rack <n>` when cards are in no rack. Clicking one shows that rack's
tiles. The first rack is chosen on first show; the choice is kept per project while the app runs.
Right of the chips: `n channels, m in use` for the shown rack (`ui_type.small`, `shell.text_2`).
One rack and no unracked cards: the chips are not drawn, only the total. Element names:
`ecad.plc.rack.<tag>`, `ecad.plc.rack.none`.

### 3.3 Tiles

The tiles of the shown rack, from `plc::overview::rack_tiles` (shared with the sheet, §4.2):

1. **The rack tile** first: `Rack` / its tag / its type (`CPU`, `IM`) / `<n> slots, <m> used` /
   its page link.
2. **One tile per slot** from the first slot to the highest occupied slot, then **one free tile**
   (the next free slot) unless the rack is full. Gaps are free tiles. Never all 999.
3. On `No rack`: one tile per card in no rack, by tag, headed `No rack`.

Tile, 112 px wide (`size.tile` 112 px, §8), ground `shell.raised`, border `shell.line`, radius
`radius.chip`, padding `space.m`, gap `space.m` between tiles; the strip scrolls sideways when wider
than the view (no wrapping):

| Line | Text | Type token | Colour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `Slot 2` (`Rack`, `No rack`); `Slot 2 · selected` when selected | `ui_type.caption` | `shell.text_3`; `shell.accent_text` when selected |
| 2 | tag `-A03`; `Free` on a free tile | `ui_type.title` | `shell.text`; `shell.text_2` for Free |

### 3.4 Channel squares

One square per channel of the card (its channel count, drawn or not), in channel order, **8 per row**
(one byte per row, Q16), 8 × 8 px, gap 3 px. States, from 045's derived states and its Checks:

| State | Square | Legend |
| --- | --- | --- |
| used | filled `shell.text_2` | in use |
| spare | outline 1 px `shell.line_strong`, no fill | free |
| reserved (a description, no wire) | filled `shell.provisional` | reserved (Q10) |
| any 045/051 Check names this channel | filled `shell.error` (wins over the others) | has a check |

Hover on a square: `-A03 channel 3 · pin 4 · Q0.3 · Pump 1 run · wired to -K11:A1` (045 §4.7's
hover). Click: selects that channel's row in the table below (filtering to the card if needed) and
scrolls to it. Double click: goes to the channel's cell on its page (or, not drawn, does nothing and
says `Channel 3 is not drawn yet.` in the status line). Squares carry no text, so they never need a
size from a text style.

A legend row under the strip lists the four states with their squares (`ui_type.small`,
`shell.text_3`).

### 3.5 The link line

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

### 3.6 `plcpage` (new command, "Make its page")

`plcpage {card, after?}`, one transaction labelled `Make card page`:

- adds a page after `after` (a page number), else after the last page on which a card of the same
  rack is drawn, else at the end of the schematic pages, titled by `plc.page.title` (§6, `{tag}
  {type} {channels}` → `-A03 DQ 16`);
- draws every channel of the card not drawn yet as cells in one row (045's `rest`), starting 40 from
  the left of the drawing area at 60 % of its height (outputs at 180°, at 40 %), and the card body
  as the power end after the last cell when the body is placed nowhere yet;
- a row longer than the drawing area wraps into further runs below, 30 apart (each with its break
  edges);
- echo: `Made page 112 "-A03 DQ 16" with -A03 channels 0–15.`; refusal: `All 16 channels of -A03
  are drawn (page 108).` MCP and the command line reach it as any command; with writes off it is
  refused (brief 011 rule).

### 3.7 The channel table

Under the strip, the I/O column set of 045c, filtered to the selected tile's card (`This card`), its
Slot, Rack, Card and Type columns hidden (the tile says them). The segmented control `This card |
All cards` in the table header switches to every card, grouped by card, as Lists ▸ I/O is today.
Selecting a row in `All cards` selects its card's tile.

**Inputs (Q11):** Description, Address and Pins are inputs (039's inline edit, set-value look);
everything else stays read (grey, no box, go-to). An edit runs 045's `plcchannel {card, channel,
text | address | pins}`; its refusal shows on the cell (039's refusal). Address shows the automatic
address as the placeholder; a typed one is marked `*`. With writes off they are read.

## 9. Update maps and cost

### 9.1 Card frames on a page (`plc::look`)

Frames are parts of the page projection, computed for one page from its runs. Recomputed for a
page when:

| Op (kind, target) | Recomputed |
| --- | --- |
| `LinkRepresentation` / `UnlinkRepresentation` / `ModifyItem` of a cell or card body on the page | the runs of that card on that page (and of a neighbour card whose run it now abuts or left) |
| `SetProperty(card, tag \| maker \| part_number \| description \| plc.rack \| plc.slot \| plc.channels \| plc.frame.show \| plc.text.*)` | the frames of that card on the pages in `by_page` of its cells and body |
| `SetProperty(rack, tag)` | its cards' captions (045 §11.1 already lists these cards) |
| text style height or weight change (031 `textstyle`, 050 `style`), `text.tag.show*` | every page with a frame: marked stale, the page on screen redrawn (031's rule for style changes) |
| a net change on `channel_of_net` | channel texts (`Spare` ↔ description) of those channels only; the frame is unchanged |
| anything else | nothing |

Only the page on screen redraws; other pages are marked stale (owner rule).

### 9.2 Lists ▸ PLC tiles

A tile's data comes from the kept `Plc` model, the Checks model and the places. After a commit:
tiles of the cards in `Plc::last_changed` are rebuilt (their squares, range, links); the Checks
model's changed rows of PLC kinds patch the squares and the check count of their cards; a page
renumber patches the links of cards in `by_page` of that page. Full build only on first show,
reload, or switching rack chips. The table below keeps 045c's map.

### 9.3 The sheets

`plcrack` and `iolist` redraw only when `Plc::last_changed` is not empty, or the Checks of their
cards changed (`plcrack` prints no checks, so not for it), or a page renumber changes a place they
print. Otherwise untouched (same as 045c).

## 11. Format proposals (for the overseer)

Pre-v1: no compatibility work. No new op, no record field, nothing in `ecad-model`.

- **FP1. Two text kinds** `address` and `channel_text` with registry entries `text.address.*`,

- **FP4 (ops recorded, not format).** A fence or a cell placed on a wire records, in its one
  transaction, the deletion of the wire pieces it trims (existing conductor ops).
- **FP5. Registry entries** `plc.frame.show`, `plc.page.title` (§6).

**Decision (overseer, 2026-11-13):** FP1–FP5 are approved.

- **FP1:** the two text kinds become text styles on 050's model once 050a lands. 051 is built

  train onto `feature/style-model`. Either way `address` and `channel_text` land as two built-in
  text styles of 050's catalogue, not as 031-only kinds.

## 13. Owner questions, each with a recommendation

1. **Cell pitch as the pack: DI and DQ cells 20 mm wide** (AI/AO 20, RTD 40, IO-Link 30, points 10
   apart within a channel); a fence across wires closer than a cell is refused with the way out.
   **Recommend yes**: it is the pitch of the pack, and it is what makes pin, channel number and
   address fit without overlap at readable sizes. (The alternative, keeping 10 mm and moving the
   address out of the cell, crowds the outside texts at 2.5 mm.)
2. **One frame per run**: cells of one card that abut are drawn as one box, with the name block
   (tag, caption, maker, part number) inside at the bottom left and the type text at the bottom
   right; derived, never stored. **Recommend yes.**
3. **Card bodies become the power end**: the body is a 20 mm cell with `L+` and `M` on the top edge;
   placed at the end of a run it is part of the card's box; placed alone it is a small framed card.
   **Recommend yes**: it is how the pack draws the supply, and no new symbol is needed.
4. **Break edges**: a run that does not hold the card's first or last channel ends in a slanted side
   on that end. **Recommend yes.**
5. **Wires end at the point**: placing cells on wires removes the wire piece inside the card in the
   same transaction; a piece that goes on to another device is kept and raises `wire-through-card`.
   **Recommend yes.**
6. **Two new text styles, `channel_text` (2.5) and `address` (2.0)**; pins stay `point_label`, the
   name block uses `tag` and `part_number`. **Recommend yes**, rather than reusing `description`
   (1.6) and changing it project-wide.
7. **Texts from bands, never shrunk**: a house style that makes texts too big for the cells gets one
   Info row per run (`plc-text-wide`), not smaller text. **Recommend yes.**
8. **Lists' I/O tab becomes PLC**, opening on the rack; the full I/O list stays one click away (`All
   cards`). **Recommend yes.**
9. **The rack strip is a generic "group strip" of 039's table** (tiles over the table's groups,
   selecting one filters the rows), not a new panel, so the Terminals and Cables lists can use it
   later. **Recommend yes.**
10. **Screen m's "provisional address" square** is shown for **reserved** channels (a description,
    no wire yet) in the provisional colour, and the legend says "reserved". 045 has no provisional
    addresses (addresses are live, pinned or typed). **Recommend yes**; if you meant "address not
    pinned yet", say so and the square shows unpinned automatic addresses instead.
11. **Description, Address and Pins editable in the channel table now** (through 045's
    `plcchannel`), as the mock-up shows them as inputs; other PLC-manager editing stays 2.8.
    **Recommend yes**: the command and the table's inline edit exist; it is small.
