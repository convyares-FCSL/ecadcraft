# Design 025: Device cross-references on a general reference index

Brief: `docs/briefs/025-device-cross-references.md` (Draft). Follows `008.md` §2 (click-first, one
commit = one JSON-equivalent transaction, Esc writes nothing, feedback in words, never crash), the
cable references of `010.md`, the devices and starter symbols of `018.md`, the frame, reference
styles and Properties panel of `019.md`, and the Checks tab of `022.md`. Format and ops proposals:
§2 (**the overseer decides**). Update map: §6. Owner questions with recommendations: §12.

## 0. What the code has today (checked on `dev` `af2d41eb`)

- **Cable references.** A reference end is a `Representation { subject: Cable(k), role:
  "reference", items: [Point] }`. Pairing and text are derived by
  `derive::cable_references(_of)`. The text is the partners' `Place` in
  `ProjectSettings.reference_style` (`105-C-4`, or `/105.C`; `?` when unlocated). Per commit,
  `references::cables_touched(doc, ops)` lists the cables an op can affect, and `None` means the
  whole document. `Snapshot::take` derives those cables before and after, and `pages_changed` drives
  redraws. `Snapshot` also keeps the members' state that 009 draws at a reference end.
- **Devices.** A device is one `Component` (property `tag`). Each symbol is a representation with
  role `main` (the first one) or its starter key (`no`, `nc`, `power3`…), joined under 018's Path
  B: a symbol instance of another embedded definition, with `connection_points`. Kinds come from
  `symbols::Kind`: `Contact { make }` (`no`, `nc`, `pbno`, `pbnc`, `sel`), `Fixed { exclusive:
  true }` (`coil`, `mcb1`, `mcb2`, `fuse`, `psu`, `lamp`, `motor3`, `motor1`, `trafo`), `Fixed {
  exclusive: false }` (`power3`), `Plc`, `Terminal` and `Tagless`. A device holds at most one
  exclusive symbol. Join labels: contacts by IEC sequence digit (NO `s3/s4`, NC `s1/s2`);
  `power3` takes `1`…`6`.
- **Indexes** (`ecad_model::index`, kept by `Op::apply`): `subject_reps`, `page_reps`,
  `item_reps`, `terminal_reps`, `terminal_ends`, `definition_components`, `tags` and
  `continuations`.
- **Views with update maps:** Checks (022, per subject), Devices tab (`device_list::devices_touched`),
  Properties (`props::touched`), page projection (`pages_touched`, with the active page drawn and
  others marked stale).
- **Bench, large tier** (500 pages, 5 000 components, 25 000 wires, 2 500 cables, release,
  2026-11-09): `derive` 13.1 ms and `xref` 9.2 ms (all cable references derived once),
  `draw_move` 1.8 ms, `edit` 0.06 ms.

## 1. Goal

Every place-showing text in a drawing (cable partners, a contact's coil, a coil's contacts) is one
**reference**. A source shows a target's place, and one index finds, for any item that moved or
whose page changed, exactly the texts that must change.

## 2. Format and ops proposals (for the overseer)

No new op. The index is derived and never serialised. Every addition is optional and left out at
its default, so an old file opens unchanged and saves back byte-identical when the feature is
unused.

**FP1. Component property `contacts`: what the part offers (recommended).** Text such as
`3P+1NO+1NC`, set with `SetProperty(Component, "contacts")` and edited in Properties (§5.2). This
follows the owner decision for v0.1 that parts are component properties. A later `.ecadlib`
definition may carry a default, and the property overrides it. Grammar (case-insensitive, `+` or
`,` between entries, spaces ignored):

| Entry | Means | Labels it offers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `3P` | n power NO poles | `1/2`, `3/4`, `5/6`… |
| `3PNC` | n power NC poles (rare) | continue the power numbering |
| `1NO`, `2NC` | n auxiliary contacts | IEC sequence digit in written order: `1NO+1NC` → `13/14`, `21/22` |
| `13/14 NO`, `21/22 NC`, `L1/T1 P` | one contact with explicit labels | as written |

At most 32 poles. Unreadable text is refused with a way forward: `"3X" is not a contact list.
Write it like 3P+1NO+1NC, or 13/14 NO, 21/22 NC.` Empty or `null` clears it, and the offer is then
unknown (§4.3).

**FP2. Component property `mirror`: where the table goes (recommended).** `auto` (absent: the project
default), `below`, `right`, `off`, or `at <dx>,<dy>`, an offset in millimetres from the head
symbol's insertion point, set by Move Mirror Table (§4.4). The values are stored on the component,
because a device has at most one head (§3.2).

**FP3. `ProjectSettings.contact_mirror` (optional).** It holds `auto` (default), `below`, `right`
or `off`, through the existing `SetProjectSettings`, and is left out at `auto`, as
`reference_style` is. It lets a designer whose house style has no mirror tables turn them off once
(Q7). It is also shown in the Project Properties dialog's Sheet group as **Contact tables**.

**Code, not format:** the starter catalogue gains each symbol's **poles** (`no`: one auxiliary NO;
`nc`: one auxiliary NC; `pbno`, `pbnc` and `sel` likewise; `power3`: three power NO) and whether it
is a **head** (every `Fixed { exclusive: true }`). The mirror glyphs are drawn by `look.rs`.

## 3. The general reference index (brief scope 1)

### 3.1 Words

- **Group:** the objects whose representations may point at each other: `Cable(CableId)` or
  `Device(ComponentId)`. A later `Name(key)` group would hold continuations (Q9).
- **Member:** a representation in a group, with a **class**: `End` (a cable reference end),
  `Head`, `Child { poles }` or `Other`. Class comes from the representation's role and from its
  symbol's definition, looked up through the starter catalogue by origin. A drawn form falls back
  to its role key. Unknown definitions are `Other`.
- **Edge:** source → target, meaning "the source shows the target's place". Edges are made by the
  group's **rule** from its members, and never stored in the file.
- **Place** of a target: `derive::Place` of its anchor (the Point item of an end; the insertion
  point of a symbol instance; the box centre of a drawn form) on its page. That is the page's shown
  number, column label and row label, written in `reference_style`.

### 3.2 Rules (the only type-specific part)

| Group | Edges | What a source shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Cable(k)` | every `End` → every other `End` of k | today's text: up to 3 partners' places, then `+N`; `?` when there is none (010 §6.2, unchanged) |
| `Device(c)` | every `Child` → the `Head`; the `Head` → every `Child` | a child: the head's place under it (§4.1). The head: its mirror table, one row per pole (§4.2) |

A device with no head gets no edges: its contacts show nothing, and a push button with two contact
blocks has no references. If a hostile file has two heads, the lower ID is the head and the other
is `Other`. **Parent and child** use the same rule: an MCB (`mcb1`, a head) with a joined `nc` is a
parent with a child (§8).

### 3.3 Structure and where it lives

```
RefIndex {
  member:  RepresentationId → (Group, Class)
  members: Group → set of RepresentationId
  targets: Multi<source, target>      // what each source shows
  sources: Multi<target, source>      // who shows each target  (the "target to reference" index)
}
```

It lives in `ecad-cadcraft` (`xref.rs`) beside Checks, not in `ecad_model::index` (Q1), because
the rule needs the starter catalogue, which `ecad-model` must not know. It is built whole on open,
reload, branch switch and revision open (O(representations of components and cable ends)). After
that, the commit, undo, redo and merge paths keep it up to date from the ops (§6), using the same
before-and-after hook as `Snapshot` today. When a group's members change, only that group's edges
are made again (O(group): at most 2 for a cable, 10 for a device under 018's 9-contact limit). Verify
mode and the debugger's **Check index** (on request, on a copy) rebuild it and compare.

### 3.4 Cable references on the index

`cables_touched` and the partner half of `Snapshot` are replaced by **targets touched** (§6): the
places that changed, plus the groups whose edges changed. The texts of their sources are derived
before and after, and the pages with a changed text are redrawn or marked stale. The text itself
is still derived by `derive::cable_references_of`, which is called for the touched groups' cables,
so every string, colour and orphan rule is unchanged (brief AC4). What 009 draws at a reference end
(members' routes and attachments) stays a per-cable snapshot. It is not a place, so it is not an
edge.

## 4. What the drawing shows (brief scope 2)

All of it is derived, drawn on `ECAD-TAGS` by `look.rs` in the active page's projection, and never
stored. Text is upright whatever the symbol's rotation. Inch projects divide every size by 25.4.

### 4.1 Under a contact

The head's place, for example `100-B-3`, in `ecad.xref` (ACI 7), 2.5 mm high.
- **Vertical contact** (its connection points share x): left-aligned, 1 mm right of the axis, with
  its top 1 mm below the lower connection point. It sits beside the wire that leaves the contact,
  under the contact. For `power3`, the rightmost pole's axis is used.
- **Horizontal contact:** centred under the symbol box, with its top 1.5 mm below the box.
- **No head:** nothing is drawn.

Picking the text selects nothing. Double-click, or **Go to Reference** (§5.3), goes to the coil.
Editing it is refused with `The reference text is derived; move the coil or the contact instead.`

### 4.2 The contact mirror table under a coil

Row form, one row per **pole**. A table is drawn when the head has children or the device has an
offer. It is not drawn for `off`, or for a head with neither.

```
          -K1  ┌──┐ A1
               │  │            (coil, page 100)
               └──┘ A2
                 │
   ──────────────┴──────────── 0V rail
            ──────────               top bar, 20 mm, centred on the axis
          1 ─┘╱ ─ 2   100-C-3    power  NO   (drawn)
          3 ─┘╱ ─ 4   100-C-3
          5 ─┘╱ ─ 6   100-C-3
                                     2 mm gap between groups
         13 ─┘╱ ─ 14  105-D-2    aux    NO
         21 ─┘╲┤─ 22  105-E-2    aux    NC
         23 ─┘╱ ─ 24  —          spare: offered, not drawn  (ecad.xref-spare)
         33 ─┘╱ ─ 34  106-A-4    over: more than offered    (ecad.orphan)
```

Geometry, in millimetres relative to the table's top centre T:

| Part | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Top bar | (−10, 0)–(10, 0), `ecad.wire-weight` |
| Row i (from 0) | middle at y = −3 − 4i (`ecad.mirror-row` 4.0); +2 mm before the first auxiliary row |
| Left label | right-aligned, ending at x = −5 |
| Glyph | x −4…4 at the row middle. NO: (−4,0)–(−1.5,0), blade (−1.5,0)–(1.5,1.5), (1.5,0)–(4,0). NC: same leads, blade (−1.5,0)–(2,−1.2), stop (1.5,0)–(1.5,−1.2) |
| Right label | left-aligned from x = 5 |
| Place | left-aligned from x = 12: the pole's place, `—` for a spare |

Text height 2.5 mm, vertically centred on the row. Power rows come first, then auxiliary rows. Each
group is ordered by the first label, compared naturally (`2` before `13`).

**Rows.** Offered poles are matched to drawn poles first by labels, then by kind, in order, for
drawings made before the offer was set. A drawn pole always shows its own labels. Unmatched offered
poles are **spare** (`ecad.xref-spare`, ACI 8, place `—`). Unmatched drawn poles are **over**
(`ecad.orphan`, ACI 1, §4.3). With no offer, every drawn pole is a plain row.

**Placement** (`mirror`, else `contact_mirror`, else `auto`):

| Value | T is |
| --- | --- |
| `auto` | on the head's axis, 4 mm below the end of a straight vertical conductor run that starts at the head's lowest connection point, which is usually the rail the coil's A2 wire meets. With no such conductor, as `below` |
| `below` | on the head's axis, 4 mm below its lowest connection point |
| `right` | 14 mm right of the head's box, level with its top |
| `at dx,dy` | the head's insertion point + (dx, dy). The table follows the coil when it moves |
| `off` | no table, while texts under contacts remain |

Picking a row selects nothing. Hovering a row shows that pole (§5.1). Double-clicking a drawn row
goes to that contact. Editing is refused with `The contact table is derived; move it with Move
Mirror Table, or set it in Properties.`

### 4.3 Counts (brief scope 3)

Per device with an offer, for each of **power NO**, **power NC**, **auxiliary NO** and **auxiliary
NC**: drawn > offered gives a Checks row, never a refusal.

| Kind | Severity | Row text | Subject, go-to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `too-many-contacts` | Warning ▲ | `-K1: 2 auxiliary NO drawn, part offers 1 (3P+1NO+1NC)` | the component; go-to opens the page of the first **over** pole and rings it |

Fewer drawn than offered is normal (spares), so it is not a check (Q8). With no offer there is no
check. A join beyond the offer still commits, and its echo says so: `Added NO contact 33/34 to -K1;
its part offers 1 auxiliary NO, now 2 drawn (see Checks).`

**Join labels follow the offer (Q4).** When `-K1` has an offer, joining a contact takes the
labels of the first free offered pole of that kind (`place`'s join step, 018 §3.1). Only when none
is free does it fall back to 018's sequence digit. This changes connection-point labels, never tags,
so it stays clear of 026.

### 4.4 Move Mirror Table (`mirrormove`)

ECAD tab, Components group, beside Device Properties (icon: an existing move glyph).
`Select contact table or coil:`, then pick a table row, its bar or the coil. Next, `Place the table
[Auto/Below/Right/Off] <pick a point>:`. The preview draws the table following the cursor. A click
commits `SetProperty(Component, "mirror", "at dx,dy")`, one transaction labelled `Move contact
table`. A typed option sets that word instead. Esc writes nothing.

## 5. Reading references (brief scope 5)

### 5.1 Hover lens (018 §6.4, new lines)

On a contact: `Coil: 100-B-3  (page 100 "Supply")`, or `Coil: not drawn` when the device has no
head. On a coil: `Contacts: 5 drawn, 1 spare (3P+1NO+1NC)`, then up to 6 rows `13/14 NO aux
105-D-2`, then `+N more`. On a table row, the lens for that pole: `-K1 13/14 NO, auxiliary, page 105
"Motor control" 105-D-2`, or `… offered, not drawn`. Cable references are unchanged (010 §8).

### 5.2 Properties panel (019 §8, device section)

| Field | For | Editing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Contacts offered** | any device | text, FP1 grammar, placeholder `e.g. 3P+1NO+1NC`; refusals under the field |
| **Contact table** | a device with a head | combo `Project default (auto)`, `Auto`, `Below`, `Right`, `Off`, and `Moved` (read-only when `at`, with a **Reset** button that sets `auto`) |
| **Contacts** (read-only) | head selected | the table's rows as text, each with **Go to** |
| **Coil** (read-only) | a contact selected | `100-B-3` with **Go to** |

Each edit is one `setprop` transaction and one `u`.

### 5.3 Go to Reference (generalising `cablegoto`)

The 010 tool now takes any source: a cable reference, a contact's text, a table row or a contact
symbol (which goes to its coil). It opens the target's page, centres it and rings it for 1.5 s with
`ecad.preview-ring`. It records nothing. JSON: `refgoto {source, target?}`. `cablegoto` stays as an
alias.

### 5.4 JSON forms and MCP

| Command | Writes | Form → result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `xrefs` | no | `{component?, cable?, page?, limit? (200, max 2000), after?}` → `{references: [{source, source_page, target, target_page, kind: "cable"\|"contact"\|"mirror", text}], next?}` |
| `contacts` | no | `{component}` → `{offer, head, placement, rows: [{labels: "13/14", kind: "NO", group: "aux"\|"power", state: "drawn"\|"spare"\|"over", representation?, place?}], over}` |
| `refgoto` | no | as §5.3 |
| `setprop` | yes | new keys `contacts` and `mirror` with §2's rules |
| `mirrormove` | yes | `{component, at?: [x,y], placement?: word}` → as §4.4 |
| `place` (join) | yes | labels from the offer (§4.3); the result adds `offer_slot: true\|false` |
| `checks` | no | lists `too-many-contacts` |

The MCP contract tests (brief 011 rule) drive `setprop` with `contacts` and `mirror`, `mirrormove` and
`place` join through `execute`, and check `Author::Agent(<client>)` and refusal with writes off. They
also check that `xrefs`, `contacts` and `refgoto` work with writes off and record nothing.

## 6. Update map (brief scope 1, `ecad/CLAUDE.md` "Derived views")

**Refresh a source** means deriving its text or table again. If it is on the active page, that
part is redrawn; otherwise its page is marked stale. **Place changed** for a target is decided by
comparing its `Place` before and after (O(1)). Every op not listed changes nothing in the index or
in any reference text. A full build happens only on open, reload, branch switch or revision open.

| Op (kind, target) | Index | Refreshed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AddItem` | nothing (an item shows only through a representation op) | — |
| `ModifyItem` / `RemoveItem` of an item of member r | nothing | r is a target whose place changed: `sources(r)`. r's own text or table moves with r (r's page is touched already) |
| `ModifyItem` / `RemoveItem` of a conductor route attached (`terminal_ends`) to a head's lowest connection point | nothing | that head's table, only when its placement is `auto` |
| `LinkRepresentation` / `UnlinkRepresentation` r, subject `Component(c)` or a cable `End` of k | member added or removed; group c or k edges made again | sources whose target set changed (texts), and targets whose source set changed (their tables, and partner texts) |
| `ModifyRepresentation` r | class and labels of r again; group edges made again | as Link, plus `sources(r)` when its place changed |
| `CreateComponent` / `DeleteComponent` | group added or removed (its representations come and go by representation ops) | — |
| `AddTerminal` / `RemoveTerminal` of a component in a group | nothing | that device's head table (labels) |
| `AttachEnd` / `CreateConductor` / `DeleteConductor` | nothing | a head table under `auto` when the conductor is on its lowest point; 009's member snapshot for the conductor's cables (as today) |
| `CreateCable` / `DeleteCable` / `AddCableMember` / `RemoveCableMember` | nothing (ends are representations) | 009's member snapshot of that cable only. **Today these return "whole document"**: the index makes them per cable |
| `SetProperty(Component c, contacts)` | rows of c | c's head table; Checks c |
| `SetProperty(Component c, mirror)` | nothing | c's head table |
| `SetProperty(Component, other keys)`, `(Conductor, …)`, `(Cable, …)`, `(Project, …)` | nothing | nothing (tags are drawn by 018's code) |
| `SetProperty(Page P, number \| sheet)` | nothing | for each member on P (`page_reps(P)` ∩ members): `sources` of it |
| `SetProperty(Page, folder)`, `AddPage`, `RemovePage`, folder ops | nothing | as above, for the pages in `book::positions_changed` (unnumbered pages only move) |
| `SetProjectSettings` changing `reference_style`, `sheet` or `length_unit` | nothing | every source: the active page is redrawn, other pages with sources are marked stale (O(index), no item scan). This is the one wide case, as in 019 §9 |
| `SetProjectSettings` changing `contact_mirror` | nothing | heads whose `mirror` is absent: as above |
| `EmbedDefinition` / `RemoveDefinition` of an existing ID | classes of `definition_components`' groups again | as Link |
| `AddLayer`, `RemoveLayer`, `NameVersion`, `UnnameVersion` | nothing | nothing |

**Other views.** For **Checks**, `too-many-contacts` is re-checked for components whose group or
`contacts` changed (it joins 022's per-subject map, under "`SetProperty` component" and
"representation ops of a component symbol"). The **Properties** section and the hover lens are
copies built on show, and refreshed when the touched set meets the selection (019 §9). The
**Devices tab** is unchanged.

## 7. Cost on the large tier

**Build** visits about 5 000 device groups (1–3 representations each in the generator) and 2 500
cable groups, about 10 000 members and 15 000 edges, with two `BTreeMap` inserts each. The
estimate is **2–5 ms**, inside the ~150 ms open budget. It runs once, after the model index. Memory
is about 1–2 MB.

**Edits:** one representation op costs O(log n) plus O(group). A coil move with 4 contacts on 3
pages costs 1 place compare, 4 source texts and 3 pages marked stale, so **well under 0.1 ms** before
drawing. A page renumber touches only the members on that page (about 60 on the large tier) and their
sources. Nothing on an ordinary edit scans the index or the document.

**Drawing** a page's tables and texts is O(sources on the page × group). A page with 10 coils
should stay inside today's `draw_window` (~2.4 ms).

**Bench (`cargo xtask bench`, large):** the generator gains 1 000 coils, each with one `power3` on
its own page and two auxiliary contacts on the next page, and offers on half of them. The cable rows
must stay comparable. New rows:
- `xbuild ms`: index build on open;
- `xmove ms`: move such a coil, commit and refresh;
- `xrenum ms`: renumber a page with 40 targets;
- `xmirror ms`: first view of a page with 10 tables.

`xref ms` (all cable texts) must not get worse. A failing row, such as `xmove` being 1 ms slower than
`draw_move`, is a reviewer finding.

## 8. Parent and child: is it cheap? (brief scope 4)

**Yes for the index.** The `Device` rule already is parent and child: any head (an exclusive
symbol) and any child (a pole-bearing symbol) of one component. **MCB with an auxiliary contact**
works in this brief at no extra cost: `-Q1` (`mcb1`) with a joined `nc` gets `21/22 NC` under the
MCB and the MCB's place under the NC. **A PLC card with channels** costs nothing in the index, but
the catalogue has no card symbol to be the head: `di` and `do` are channels only. It needs a `plc`
card starter (a head whose table is a channel list `I0.0 105-C-2`) and a channel row form. That is a
small later step that adds a catalogue entry and a table form, with no index change (Q10).

## 11. Quick start: section "5K. Cross-references (by clicking)"

On `demo.ecad`, after 5J. The worker checks the page numbers against the demo.

1. **A coil and its contacts.** On page 100, Find Symbol `coil`, click to place `-K1`, then wire its
   A2 straight down to a rail. Find `power3`, press `D`, accept `-K1` and place it on 100.
2. On page 105, place a `no` and an `nc` and join both to `-K1`. Under each contact you see
   `100-B-3` (the coil's place). Back on 100, the table under the rail lists five rows with their
   places.
3. **Follow a reference.** Double-click `100-B-3` under the NC: page 100 comes up with the coil
   ringed. Double-click the table's `21/22` row: back to the NC.
4. **The part's contacts.** Select the coil. In Properties set **Contacts offered** to
   `3P+2NO+1NC` and press Enter. A grey `23/24 —` spare row appears. Type `u`, then set it again.
5. **One too many.** Join two more `no` contacts on 105. The first takes `23/24`, and the second is
   listed red in the table and in the **Checks** tab. Click the Checks row to go to it. Type `u`.
6. **Move the table.** Click **Move Mirror Table**, click the table, then click a new place. Move the
   coil: the table follows. In Properties set **Contact table** to `Off`, then `Project default`.
7. **Renumber.** Set page 105's number to `106`: the two auxiliary rows read `106-…`.

§9 (limitations) gains: no PLC card symbol yet; no change-over (`CO`) contacts; contact texts and
tables can be moved per device only (texts stay automatic); continuations are not on the index yet.

## 12. Owner questions, each with a recommendation

1. **Where the index lives:** `ecad-cadcraft` (`xref.rs`), kept per commit beside Checks, or
   `ecad_model::index`, kept by `Op::apply`? **Recommend `ecad-cadcraft`**: its rule needs the
   starter catalogue, which the model must not know. It still never rescans, and verify mode compares
   it with a rebuild.
2. **Head rule:** the head is the device's exclusive symbol (coil, MCB, motor…). A device without
   one shows no references and no check. **Recommend yes**: push buttons and selectors have no head
   and need none.
3. **Offer storage (FP1):** component property `contacts` in the `3P+1NO+1NC` form, rather than a
   typed field or a definition field now? **Recommend the property**: parts are properties in v0.1,
   it needs no op, and `.ecadlib` can supply the default later.
4. **Join labels follow the offer (§4.3):** a joined NO takes the part's next free NO labels
   (`23/24`) before 018's sequence digit. **Recommend yes**: the part fixes its labels, and only
   connection-point labels change, never tags (026 is untouched).
5. **Table form:** row form (labels, a horizontal glyph, place) rather than the column form with
   vertical glyphs? **Recommend row form**: it stays readable with ten or more poles and fits one
   50 mm column.
6. **Default placement `auto`:** under the rail that the coil's A2 wire meets, else under the coil.
   **Recommend yes**: in your reference the table sits under the coil, clear of the wire. `below`,
   `right` and `off` cover other house styles.
7. **Project default `contact_mirror` (FP3):** add it now? **Recommend yes**: one optional settings
   field, left out at default, as `reference_style` is. Per-device `mirror` covers the exceptions.
8. **Counts:** only drawn > offered is a Warning, and spares are no check. **Recommend yes**:
   spares are normal. A "spare contacts" list belongs to the outputs (024).
9. **Continuations (022) on the index:** a `Name(key)` group with all-to-all edges would remove
   the remaining whole-document case in `pages_touched`. **Recommend a later step after 026
   lands**, because lane C is in `connectivity.rs` now.
10. **PLC card:** a later step adding a `plc` card starter and a channel table, with no index change
    (§8). **Recommend yes, after 028.**
11. **Contact text position:** under the contact, just right of its lower wire, rather than beside
    the symbol. **Recommend yes**: it matches the roadmap's "under contacts" and keeps clear of the
    tag on the left.
12. **Old projects:** existing coils and contacts gain texts and tables on open, with no
    transaction. **Recommend accept**: it is derived, like 019's frame.
